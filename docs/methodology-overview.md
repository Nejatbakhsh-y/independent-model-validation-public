# Independent Model Validation Methodology Overview

## 1. Purpose

Independent validation provides structured challenge of a model's design, implementation, empirical performance, limitations, and fitness for its intended use.

## 2. Independence and Scope

The review considers the stated model purpose, available evidence, validation scope, materiality, and limitations.

An actual engagement requires appropriate independence safeguards and clearly defined responsibilities.

## 3. Validation Domains

### Conceptual Soundness

Assess methodological suitability, assumptions, specification, design rationale, and alignment with intended use.

### Data Quality

Assess data relevance, completeness, representativeness, integrity, and material limitations.

### Implementation Consistency

Examine consistency between the documented methodology and the implemented model, within the agreed review scope.

### Quantitative Performance

Evaluate predictive performance, relevant statistical metrics, stability, and model behavior using appropriate evaluation procedures.

### Sensitivity and Robustness

Examine material assumptions, performance sensitivity, and robustness under relevant scenarios.

### Benchmarking

Consider reasonable benchmark or challenger approaches when relevant to the model and available evidence.

### Limitations and Model Risk

Document material limitations, uncertainty, potential misuse, and risks affecting intended use.

### Governance and Documentation

Review documentation sufficiency, traceability, and the evidence required to support validation conclusions.

## 4. Findings and Remediation

Findings should describe the issue, supporting evidence, potential impact, severity rationale, and recommended remediation.

## 5. Validation Reporting

Typical deliverables may include:

- Independent Model Validation Report
- Executive Validation Summary
- Model Risk Findings Register
- Prioritized Remediation Recommendations
- Findings Presentation
- Follow-Up Remediation Review

## 6. Validation Disposition

A validation conclusion should be evidence-based, appropriately scoped, and consistent with the model's intended use and unresolved material findings.

Final disposition remains subject to applicable organizational governance and approval authority.

## 7. Demonstration Disclaimer

This overview accompanies a demonstration package. It does not establish that a real-client validation engagement has occurred or that regulatory approval has been obtained.
